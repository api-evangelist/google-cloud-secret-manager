Feature Creating regional secrets using Secret Manager is now in Generally Availability ( GA ). Regional secrets let you store your sensitive data within a specific geographic location, ensuring it remains in that region at all times – whether at rest, in use, or in transit. Regional secrets are crucial for meeting data residency requirements and complying with regulatory mandates.
