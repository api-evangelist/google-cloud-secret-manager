Feature The Secret Manager add-on for Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) now supports the automatic rotation of secrets. You can configure the Secret Manager add-on to automatically rotate secrets so that secrets updated in Secret Manager after initial pod deployment are automatically and periodically pushed to the pod. This feature is available in Preview . For more information, see Configure automatic rotation of secrets .
